\section*{NeurIPS Paper Checklist}

\begin{enumerate}

\item {\bf Claims}
    \item[] Question: Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: The abstract and introduction clearly state the knapsack formulation, empirical results on HotpotQA, NQ, and MEM1 comparison, and explicitly scope limitations including the GBDT scorer label leakage and sample size.

\item {\bf Limitations}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work performed by the authors?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: Section~6 is dedicated to discussing limitations, explicitly analyzing the scorer-label training overlap, small sample size on WebShop ($N=5$), and the requirement for larger disjoint benchmarks.

\item {\bf Theory Assumptions and Proofs}
    \item[] Question: For each theoretical result, does the paper provide the full set of assumptions and a complete proof?
    \item[] Answer: \answerNA{}
    \item[] Justification: The paper formulates an empirical systems optimization problem and does not state new mathematical theorems or formal proofs.

\item {\bf Experimental Result Reproducibility}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper fully disclose all information needed to reproduce the experimental results?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: Section~4, Section~5, and Appendix~A--E provide complete specifications of baseline algorithms, token budgets, exact feature extraction equations, prompt templates, and execution parameters.

\item {\bf Open Access to Data and Code}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper describe how the data and code can be accessed by other researchers?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: All experiment runner scripts, benchmark dataset loaders, and trained model weights will be released publicly on GitHub under an open-source license upon publication (anonymized during review).

\item {\bf Experimental Setting and Compute Resources}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper describe the experimental setting and compute resources used?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: Section~4, Appendix~A, Appendix~B, and Appendix~C report model identities (Qwen3.6-27B on Groq), local CPU execution specs, token budget constraints, and latency profiles.

\item {\bf Existing Assets}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper cite the creators of existing assets used?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: Section~4 and Appendix~A cite existing benchmark datasets (HotpotQA, Natural Questions, WebShop) and model architectures.

\item {\bf New Assets}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper mention the license and intended use of new assets created?
    \item[] Answer: \answerYes{}
    \item[] Justification: We release our modular CSC compilation pipeline, evaluation suite, and pretrained GBDT utility scorer under the MIT License for open research use.

\item {\bf Crowdsourcing and Human Subjects}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper describe human participant procedures if applicable?
    \item[] Answer: \answerNA{}
    \item[] Justification: No human subjects or crowdsourcing were involved; all benchmarking was conducted using automated evaluation pipelines.

\item {\bf Institutional Review Board (IRB) Approvals}
    \item[] Question: Does the paper describe IRB approvals if applicable?
    \item[] Answer: \answerNA{}
    \item[] Justification: No human participant data or personally identifiable information was gathered.

\end{enumerate}
